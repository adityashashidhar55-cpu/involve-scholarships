Postgrad Solutions Study Bursary

If you are starting a postgraduate course in 2025 you are eligible to apply for a Postgrad Solutions Study Bursary. Each bursary is worth £2,000 and they are open to students from anywhere in the world. Please see Postgrad Solutions Study Bursary for more details.
