WHITE Spot Limited Bursary

Campus: Vancouver and non-UBC students Two $500 bursaries are offered by White Spot Limited, sponsored through supplier partnerships, for their employees and sons and daughters of their employees who have served with White Spot for at least one year by the application deadline. The bursaries are open to students in a full program of studies at the University of B.C., the University of Victoria, Vancouver Island University, Simon Fraser University or the British Columbia Institute of Technology. Candidates must have achieved an average of at least 65% in their previous year of study. The awards
