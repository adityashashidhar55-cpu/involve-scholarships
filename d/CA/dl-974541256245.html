School of Nursing 25th Reunion Award

Established in March 1999 by the Class of Nursing Science 1969 on the occasion of their 25th reunion and revised in June 2015. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to a student in any year of the Bachelor of Nursing program in the Faculty of Health Sciences. Preference will be given to students in the Advanced Standing Track.
