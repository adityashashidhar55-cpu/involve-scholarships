Elkhorn Manitoba Award

Established in October 2013 and revised in March 2019 by Ryan Pedlow, BCom 1998. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students entering the first year of the Bachelor of Commerce program in the Smith School of Business. First preference will be given to students from the province of Manitoba and second preference will be given to students from the province of Saskatchewan. The award is renewable for one subsequent year provided a satisfactory academic average is maintained and financial need remains evident.
