Mary and James FYFE-SMITH Memorial Bursary

Through a bequest of the late Florence Fyfe-Smith, three bursaries of $1,500 each are awarded to Indigenous students entering or in attendance respectively; as to the first, in the School of Social Work and Family Studies or Nursing, as to the second in the Faculty of Education and as to the third, in the Peter A. Allard School of Law.
