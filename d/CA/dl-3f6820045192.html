William MCMAHAN Scholarship

Campus: Vancouver and Okanagan Required documentation: A letter from the head office confirming eligibility for this award is required. A scholarship of $2,850, endowed by William McMahan, Esq., Vancouver, is offered to students entering their second, third, or final year in chemical, civil, electrical, or mechanical engineering, or in forestry. This scholarship is open to sons and daughters of employees of the logging or pulp operation, or head office of Canadian Forest Products Ltd., or, failing a suitable candidate from these operations, to sons and daughters of employees in other operation
