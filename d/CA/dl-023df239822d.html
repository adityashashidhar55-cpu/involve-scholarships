Commerce 1996 Entrance Scholarship

Established in February 2007 by the members of the class of Commerce 1996 in recognition of the 10th anniversary of their graduation. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence and demonstrated community leadership to students entering the first year of the Bachelor of Commerce program in the Smith School of Business at Queen's University.
