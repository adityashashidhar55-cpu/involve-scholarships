Queen's LSS Indigenous Entrance Award

Established in May 2016 by Queen's Law Students' Society. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students entering first year of the J.D. program or combined J.D. degree program in the Faculty of Law. First preference will be given to students who have applied in the Indigenous Category. Second preference will be given to students who have applied through the Access Category. Selection will be made by the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid and Awards, in consultation with the Assistant Dean of Students of the Faculty of Law.
