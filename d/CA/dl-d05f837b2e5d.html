The Towie Scholarship

Through the generosity of an anonymous donor, this renewable award was established to enable young Indigenous Canadians to fulfill their dreams and inspire others within their communities. Two awards of $20,000 each will be offered to undergraduate students, beginning in their second year, who: 1. have self-declared as First Nations, Métis or Inuit people from Canada; 2. have successfully completed their first year (minimum 24 credit hours) of an undergraduate degree program in any faculty, college, or school at the University of Manitoba; 3. have achieved a minimum degree grade point average
