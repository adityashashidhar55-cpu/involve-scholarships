Herbert and Stella Overton Award in Drama

Established in May 1996 and last revised in October 2019 by their daughter, Isabel Bader, LLD 2007. Awarded on the basis of outstanding achievement in non-major DAN School theatrical productions to students entering fourth year of a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) degree with a plan offered by the DAN School of Drama and Music. Selection will be made by the Faculty of Arts and Science Awards Committee.
