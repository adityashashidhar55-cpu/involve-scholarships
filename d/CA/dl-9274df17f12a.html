Named Bader College Bursaries

The following bursaries and awards have been made available thanks to the generosity of Queen’s donors. If a student is selected to receive a Bader College Bursary the name of their bursary may be amended to one of the following awards. There are no separate applications for any of the named Bader College bursaries. Only the name of the award will change after a student has been selected; the value of their Bader College Bursary assistance will not change depending on the name of the award.
