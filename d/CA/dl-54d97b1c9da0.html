Susan Elizabeth Phillips Access Scholarship

Established in October 2014 by the Estate of Susan Elizabeth Phillips and awarded on the basis of academic achievement to students entering first year in the School of Medicine in the Faculty of Health Sciences. Consideration will be given to an applicant's educational and financial disadvantage, membership in a historically disadvantaged group or any other factor relating to educational barriers faced by the applicant or to the ability of the applicant to enrich the diversity of the medical school community and the medical profession. Applicants must supply supporting documentation as defined
