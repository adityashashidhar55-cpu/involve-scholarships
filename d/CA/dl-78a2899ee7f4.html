Nini M. Harris-Lowe Bursary for Chronic Conditions and Disability

Bursaries totalling $2,500 have been made available through an endowment established by Mr. Rick Lowe (B.Sc. 1982) and matched by the University of British Columbia, in memory of his spouse, Mrs. Nina M. “Nini” Harris-Lowe, for students entering or pursuing any eligible degree program who cope with chronic pain, chronic conditions or illness, disabilities or any other impairment that impacts their university studies. Recommendations are made by Enrolment Services in consultation with Access and Diversity.
