John and Margaret Gilchrist Award

Established in November 1972 by the Estate of John Gilchrist and last revised in July 2023. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students entering first year of any first-entry undergraduate degree program who graduated from Blenheim District High School. If the preceding condition cannot be met, the award may be granted on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students in any year of any Faculty or School at Queen's University who graduated from Blenheim District High School.
