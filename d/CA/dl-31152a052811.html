Dean’s International Award Program (no application required)

International students who are entering undergraduate programs at Queen’s University will be considered automatically for the Dean’s International Award program. Awarded on the basis of academic achievement to international students entering the first year of any first-entry degree program in the Faculty of Arts and Science, Stephen J. R. Smith School of Engineering and Applied Science, Smith School of Business (Bachelor of Commerce), or Faculty of Health Sciences (Bachelor of Health Sciences). The award is renewable for 3 subsequent years provided the student continues to be registered full-t
