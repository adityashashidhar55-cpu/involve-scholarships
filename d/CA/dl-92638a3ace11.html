James Robert Grasse Award in Civil Engineering

Established in November 2002 from the estate of James Robert Grasse, B.Sc. 1961, and awarded on the basis of financial need and academic achievement to a student in the second year in the Department of Civil Engineering in the Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science. The award is renewable in the third and fourth year provided the recipient maintains a GPA of 2.0 in each year and financial need remains evident.
