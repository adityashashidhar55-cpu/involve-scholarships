SIKH Students’ Association Scholarship

Campus: Vancouver and Okanagan Required documentation: A letter of recommendation from a local Sikh Temple President is required. In order to encourage and recognize individual excellence, the Sikh Students’ Association has endowed a scholarship of $500. The funds for this scholarship were donated by the Sikh community. The Sikh Students’ Association was established in 1985 to create a greater awareness of the Sikh religion and culture as well as to unite Sikh students attending post-secondary educational institutions in Canada.
