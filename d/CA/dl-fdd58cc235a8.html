Norman, Ian and Lorna Rogers Scholarship

Established by the estate of Arthur W. McLeod Rogers in 1991 and revised in December 2013. Awarded annually at the close of each academic year to an Alma Mater Society (AMS) member who has shown the most outstanding leadership in student life during such academic year. Selection will be made by a committee chaired by a member of the Senate Committee on Scholarships and Student Aid, and comprising a representative from the AMS Executive, a member of the AMS Assembly, the Rector, and a representative from the Office of the Vice-Provost and Dean of Student Affairs, or their delegates.
