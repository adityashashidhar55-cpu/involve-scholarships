The Warmbold Award

Established in October 2011 and revised in May 2014 by Benita M. Warmbold, B.Com. 1980, and Gerald N. Wood. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence, proven leadership skills, and involvement in school or community activities to a female student entering third year of the Bachelor of Commerce Program in the Smith School of Business who has chosen to concentrate her studies in the area of Finance. The award is renewable for the fourth year provided the student maintains a minimum GPA of 3.5 in the subsequent year, and involvement in leadership and community activities remains evident. Select
