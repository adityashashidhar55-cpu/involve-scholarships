Rolf Lund Award

Established by friends and colleagues and awarded to a full-time upper year student on the basis of financial need and a minimum GPA of 2.7, who has completed a minimum of one full year with the Track and Field and/or Cross Country team. A general bursary application, along with two letters of reference, should be submitted to Financial Aid and Awards. Selection will be made in consultation with the Queen's track Booster Club
