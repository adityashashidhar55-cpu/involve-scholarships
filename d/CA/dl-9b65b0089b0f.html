Michele Mainland Memorial Medal in Chemical Engineering

Established in memory of Michele Mainland, B.Sc. 1997, by family, friends, and fellow students in recognition of Michele's efforts and courage. Awarded to a graduating Chemical Engineering student who, in the opinion of classmates and department staff, best exemplifies Michele's personal qualities: persistence and cheerfulness in the face of adversity, a sense of adventure, tenacity, courage and helpfulness to others.
