Dean's Special Award

Up to ten scholarships to be awarded in the fall to Arts and Science students returning from international exchange programs. To be eligible, students must be returning to Queen's and must submit a statement of no more than 500 words describing the ways in which the exchange experience enriched their lives and affected their outlook both academically and personally. The Awards Committee of the Faculty of Arts and Science will make decisions based on this statement and on the applicant's prior academic record.
