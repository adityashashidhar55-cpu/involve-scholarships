Dr. W. B. F. Mackay Memorial Scholarship in Mechanical and Materials Engineering

Established in May 2007 by family, friends, colleagues and students of the late Dr. William Brydon Fraser Mackay, HD.Sc (1993). Awarded to a student in the fourth year of the Materials Option in the Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering on the basis of academic excellence and contributions to furthering the well-being of the student body by way of active participation in volunteer activities, project teams, and professional and social activities within the Department.
