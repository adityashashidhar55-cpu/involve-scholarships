Iranian Student Memorial Award

Campus: Vancouver and Okanagan Required documentation: Please provide a statement explaining how you meet the award criteria. Include any documentation proving Persian or Iranian heritage, or interest in the field or proof of study in the field. Awards totalling $5,400 have been made available through an endowment established by the Iranian-Canadian community and the University of British Columbia, in memory of those who perished aboard Flight PS752 in January 2020, for outstanding undergraduate and graduate students of Persian or Iranian heritage, or who are studying Persian Studies.
