The Ron Harris Bursary

The North Bay and District Pharmacists’ Association have established a bursary in memory of Ron Harris, a local pharmacist. This bursary will be awarded to a deserving and needy student enrolled in the Doctor of Pharmacy program, preferably from Northern Ontar
