Métis Nation of Ontario Student Bursary

Established by the Métis Nation of Ontario through the Métis Training Initiatives and awarded on the basis of financial need to a Métis student registered in any year in any faculty or school at Queen's University. A General Bursary application on SOLUS should be submitted by October 31. Eligible applicants will be contacted by the Financial Aid and Awards Office to complete a Métis Nation of Ontario verification form. Recipients will be selected in consultation with the Métis Nation of Ontario.
