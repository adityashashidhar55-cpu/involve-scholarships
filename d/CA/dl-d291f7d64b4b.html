Ivan T. Beck Research Prize in Digestive Diseases

Established in November 2000 by Ivan T. Beck, Emeritus Professor, Faculty of Health Sciences, to stimulate physicians to become Clinical Investigators in the field of Digestive Diseases. The prize will be awarded to a competitor for the best research study carried out during the year in the field of gastrointestinal physiology, pathophysiology or clinical investigation.
