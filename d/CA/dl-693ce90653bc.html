Sutton International Study Award

Established in December 1997 and revised in February 2015 by Gerald D. Sutton, B.Com. 1948. M.Com. 1949, and Margaret (Scally) Sutton, B.A.(Hon.) 1948, in order to foster a greater understanding of global issues. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to undergraduate or graduate students in enrolled in the Smith School of Business to enable study abroad. In the case of a graduate student, the funds are available for research study abroad.
