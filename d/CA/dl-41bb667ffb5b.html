Fallah Family Bursary

Established in September 2017 by Fariborz Fallah, B.Sc. 1987, M.Sc. 1990, and Catherine Berka, B.Sc. 1987. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students in any year in Smith Engineering at Queen's University. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by October 31. Value: variable.
