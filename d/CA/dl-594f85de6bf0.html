Dean Smith Applied Science Bursary

Established in April 2008 by Dean Smith, B.Sc. 1981, and awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students in the second year in the Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering in Smith Engineering. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by October 31.
