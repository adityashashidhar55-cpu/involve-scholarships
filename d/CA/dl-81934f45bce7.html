Dr. Chika Oriuwa Award for the Advancement of Black Health

The award supports undergraduate student(s) in the Temerty Faculty of Medicine. Awards will be administered to student(s) in their final year of studies in the MD Program, with preference given to a student(s) who has demonstrated a dedication to advancing heath in the Black community.
