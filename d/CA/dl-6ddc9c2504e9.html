The M. Carolyn Braiden Scholarship

The M. Carolyn Braiden Scholarship is awarded to a student enrolled in the Doctor of Pharmacy program who obtains First Class Honours in the annual examinations of the Third Year, and who has not received an award of equal or greater value.
