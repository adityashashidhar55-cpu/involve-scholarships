Timothy Yeung Bursary for Students with Disabilities #103155

Bursaries totalling $2,000 have been made available annually through a gift from Timothy Yeung (B.Com. 2010) for students in the UBC Sauder School of Business with disabilities. Preference will be given to students in the Business Technology Management option. Timothy received an M.B.A. from the Quantic School of Business and Technology and is currently the Director of Product Management at Hootsuite. He was involved with organizations such as EY Canada, where he served as Associate Director of Product Management and TELUS Digital as Lead Product Manager. Beyond his professional accomplishment
