The Leonard G. Berry Memorial Bursary

Established in April 1983 by family, colleagues, friends, and former students in memory of Professor Leonard G. Berry, professor of mineralogy at Queen's University from 1944 to 1980, as a tribute to his outstanding contribution to mineralogical science and his devotion as a teacher. Last revised in July 2022. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students in any year of any Faculty or School at Queen's University.
