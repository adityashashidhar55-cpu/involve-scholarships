Gordon A. Snider Prize in the History of Technology

Established by the estate of Arthur W. McLeod Rogers in 1991 and revised in December 2013. Awarded annually at the close of each academic year to an Alma Mater Society (AMS) member who has shown the most outstanding leadership in student life during such academic year. Nomination letters should be submitted to the Office of the Registrar, Financial Aid and Awards by 1 February. To apply please submit an Award with Separate Application form.
