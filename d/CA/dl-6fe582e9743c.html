James A. (Jim) Bennett Memorial Award

Established in November 2000 in memory of James Alfred (Jim) Bennett, B.Sc. (Eng) 1958, M.Sc.(Eng) 1960, Ph.D. 1965 Philosophy (Univ. of Michigan), by his family, friends, students, colleagues, and the Rotary Clubs of Kingston. Awarded annually to a student who is in the fourth year of a Smith Engineering undergraduate degree program and in financial need. The award will honour Dr. Bennett's memory by recognizing a student's service to the Kingston community and his or her leadership/example at Queen's University while maintaining a minimum GPA of 2.0 on the work of his or her program.
