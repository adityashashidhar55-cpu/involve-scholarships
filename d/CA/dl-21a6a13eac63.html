Carol Black Family Award in Engineering

Established in March 2021 in honour of Carol Black by her son, Shane Kovacs, BSc1996. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students entering the first year of a Bachelor of Applied Science degree program in Smith Engineering. Preference will be given to students from Simcoe County. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by February 15 Value: variable.
