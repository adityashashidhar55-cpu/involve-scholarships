GOVERNOR GENERAL'S GOLD MEDAL

For more than 140 years, the Governor General’s Gold Medals have recognized the outstanding scholastic achievements of students in Canada. They are awarded to master’s or doctoral students who have achieved the highest academic standing in their degree program
