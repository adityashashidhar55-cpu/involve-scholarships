Smith 30% Club EMBA Scholarship

Established in June 2015 by the Smith School of Business. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence, proven leadership skills, and commitment to the mentoring and development of senior women leaders. Awarded to female students entering the Executive MBA program in the Smith School of Business. Application is by letter by June 15. Selection will be made by the Awards Committee of the Executive MBA program in the Smith School of Business.
