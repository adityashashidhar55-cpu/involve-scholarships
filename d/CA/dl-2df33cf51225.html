The John Roberts Scholarship

The John Roberts Scholarship and Medal is awarded to the student in the Fourth Year of the Doctor of Pharmacy program who obtains the highest number of Honours grades in the Year 4 courses. If there is a tie, the cumulative GPA will be considered.
