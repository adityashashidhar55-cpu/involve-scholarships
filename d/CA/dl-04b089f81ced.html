Elizabeth and Diana MCMANUS Memorial Bursary

Campus: Vancouver Required documentation: Submit documentation of Branch No. 48 membership (i.e. a letter from Branch No. 48, Royal Canadian Legion). One or more bursaries totalling $1,900, provided by a bequest from the late William McManus, are awarded to the sons, daughters or legal dependents of members of Branch No. 48, Royal Canadian Legion, or failing suitable candidates, to a student or students in any year and faculty. The recipients must have good academic standing and be in need of financial assistance.
