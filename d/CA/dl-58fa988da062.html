Dr. George L. Edgett Memorial Scholarship in Statistics

Established in March 1989 by family, friends, and colleagues in memory of Dr. George L. Edgett who began the teaching of statistics at Queen's University in 1933. Last revised in February 2026. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence to upper year students in a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) or a Bachelor of Science (Honours) degree program with an academic plan in Statistics, offered by the Department of Mathematics and Statistics.
