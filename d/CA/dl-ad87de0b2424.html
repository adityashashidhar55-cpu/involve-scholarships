Chown Centennial Scholarship

Chown Centennial Scholarship recipients are nominated by high schools. Students do not need to apply directly. All high schools in Manitoba and Northwestern Ontario are invited to select a recipient of the Chown Centennial Scholarship, currently valued at $1000 per student. This award is to be offered to the best "all-around" student graduating from the high school and selected according to criteria established by each school.
