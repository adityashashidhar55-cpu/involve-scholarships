Stephen J. R. Smith Bursary

Established by Stephen J.R. Smith, B.Sc. (Eng) 1972, M.Sc. (Econ). Awarded to students enrolled in an Economics program in the Faculty of Arts and Science or an Electrical Engineering program in Smith Engineering on the basis of financial need, leadership, creativity, and extra-curricular activities. Preference will be given to students who have not received other awards greater than $1,000.
