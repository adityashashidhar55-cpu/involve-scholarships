Dave Black Scholarship in Science and Technology Management

Established in February 2002 by friends and colleagues in memory of Dave Black, member of the School of Business MBA for Science & Technology Advisory Board. The scholarship is aimed at those who have shown entrepreneurial initiatives and who may be future drivers in the area of technology industries. Awarded annually to a student entering second, third or fourth year of the undergraduate Commerce program on the basis of academic performance, entrepreneurial experience in the area of technology, and leadership within the Queen's community.
