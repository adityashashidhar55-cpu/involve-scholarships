Margaret K. Stevens Admission Award

Established in February 1998 by Mrs. Margaret K. Stevens Keyser (BNSc 1947) to commemorate the first graduating class of Queen’s University School of Nursing, of which she was one of only two students enrolled. Last revised in April 2026. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic excellence to a student entering the first year of a Bachelor of Nursing Science degree program.
