Flying Officer Reverend George Robert PRINGLE Memorial Bursary

Campus: Vancouver Bursaries totalling $2,800 have been endowed by friends and associates, in memory of the late Flying Officer Reverend George Robert Pringle, a much beloved graduate of outstanding Christian character and athletic ability who was killed on January 24th, 1943, while on active service overseas. It is awarded to a student who has completed two years at this University and has registered at the University for further study. The student must show evidence of academic ability, sterling, unselfish character, and active participation and leadership in University sport.
