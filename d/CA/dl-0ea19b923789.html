ILWU Local 517 Bud Smith Scholarship

Campus: Vancouver, Okanagan, and non-UBC students A $550 scholarship has been endowed by Local 517 of the ILWU in memory of its former secretary, “Bud” Smith. The scholarship is open to members in good standing of Local 517, and their sons and daughters. Candidates may attend the University of B.C., the University of Victoria, Simon Fraser University, the B.C. Institute of Technology or any college in B.C. and must enrol in a full program of undergraduate studies. The donor reserves the right to re-award the scholarship if the winner receives other scholarships of substantial value.
