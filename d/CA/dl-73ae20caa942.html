Arlene Leder (Halpern) Memorial Scholarship in Pharmacy

This award was created in honor and memory of Arlene (Halpern) Leder, a proud graduate of the Class of 7T1. Arlene was an Ontario Scholar who was admitted directly from high school into the School of Pharmacy. She spent much of her career as a pharmacist working in a medical center. Arlene’s husband
