Ernest Martin "Cappy" Rix Award

Dr. Donald B. Rix established this award in honour of his father, Ernest Martin “Cappy” Rix, a graduate of the Class of 2T7. This bursary will be awarded to Second Year Doctor of Pharmacy students who demonstrate financial need.
