Peloton Capital Management Access Bursary

Established in December 2024 by Peloton Capital Management and awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students in any year of the Bachelor of Commerce degree program at the Smith School of Business, with preference given to students entering first year. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid and Awards.
