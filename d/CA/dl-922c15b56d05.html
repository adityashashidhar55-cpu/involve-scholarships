Queen Elizabeth Scholarship

Established in May 2025 by Queen's University with funding from the Queen Elizabeth Scholars Program. Awarded to undergraduate students from Smith Engineering, the Faculty of Arts and Science, or Faculty of Health Science to undertake 8 to 12 week international community-based resilience projects. Graduate students from partner institutions will undertake one semester at Queen's University. Awards will be granted for tuition fees, living and travel-related expenses associated with an approved international study experience.
