Don Lindsay – Teck Award

Established in May 2024 by Teck Resources Limited in honour of Don Lindsay, BSc 1980. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence to students entering second year of a Bachelor of Applied Science degree program in Mining Engineering. Preference will be given to students who demonstrate community involvement or extracurricular activities. The award is renewable for 2 subsequent years provided a satisfactory academic average is maintained each year and students remain enrolled in full-time study.
