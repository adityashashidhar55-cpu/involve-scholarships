Hon. Vim Kochhar and CFPDP Award for Students with Disabilities

Awards totalling $3,500 have been made available through an endowment established by the Canadian Foundation for Physically Disabled Persons (CFPDP), in honour of The Honourable Vim Kochhar, along with matching funds from the University of British Columbia, for students with disabilities. Preference will be given to students with physical disabilities. With generous support from the Shah Family Foundation, this award has been created as a part of CFPDPs A Seat at the Table (SATT) program.
