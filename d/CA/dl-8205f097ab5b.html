Rehabilitation Therapy Class of 1970 Bursary

Established in May 2007 by members of the Rehabilitation Therapy Class of 1970 in honour of their 35th Reunion and awarded on the basis of financial need to students in the second year of the M.Sc.OT and M.Sc.PT program in the School of Rehabilitation Therapy, School of Graduate Studies and Research. Open to Canadian citizens and Permanent Residents.
