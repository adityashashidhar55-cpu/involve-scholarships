Science 1997 Bursary

Established by the Class of Science 1997 as their graduating gift to Smith Engineering at Queen's University. Awarded on the basis of financial need to one upper-year student enrolled in Smith Engineering whose contribution to extra-curricular activities or community service exemplifies the Queen's Engineering Society motto of "Renowned Spirit Unrivalled Excellence". Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by October 31.
