Kingston Symphony Association Scholarship in Music

Five scholarships which include the James Rini Memorial Scholarship and the Helen Lucile Allan Memorial Scholarship are provided annually by the Kingston Symphony Association for award to full-time students in the B.Mus. program whose major instrument is an orchestral one. The scholarships are awarded on the recommendation of the DAN School of Drama and Music on basis of a competitive audition and satisfactory academic standing.
