Russell Payson and Peggy Zafiris Award

Established in January 2022 by Russell Payson, BSc 1967, and Peggy Zafiris. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students entering first year of a Bachelor of Applied Science degree program in Smith Engineering. The award is renewable for three subsequent years provided a satisfactory academic average is maintained each year and financial need remains evident. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid.
