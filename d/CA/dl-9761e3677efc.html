Barbara A. Kwant Scholarship in French Language and Literature

One or more awards will be made annually to New College students majoring or specializing in French. Eligible applicants must be in good academic standing and have demonstrated financial need as determined by OTSS guidelines. Students are automatically considered for this award in September.
