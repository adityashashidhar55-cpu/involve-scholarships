Meds’74 Education Travel Bursary

Established in February 2026 in honour of the Class of Medicine 1974. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need for students entering their fourth year of medical school at Queen's. The bursary is intended to assist with the cost of travel and added expenses related to the completion of required electives at medical schools external to Queen's, including Canada and international locations. Preference will be given to students doing electives outside of Ontario.
