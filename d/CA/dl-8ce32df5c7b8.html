Peter Morrin Prize in Nephrology

Established in April 2002 by colleagues and friends upon Dr. Peter Morrin's retirement from the School of Medicine, Division of Nephrology, to recognize his many years of collaboration and leadership in clinical Nephrology. Awarded annually to a student in the fourth medical year obtaining the highest overall standing in Nephrology, or in medical ethics related to nephrology. Students who have not completed the nephrology clinical rotation may submit a paper prepared on medical ethics in nephrology.
