Michael McAdam and Wendy Smith Bursary

This bursary is for a returning full-time student in any Faculty, School or College who has demonstrated qualities of entrepreneurial knowledge gained at the University by developing a business venture, a product, or a service outside the University. Eligible applicants must have demonstrated financial need and achieved a minimum cumulative grade point average of 3.0. Application deadline: October 1
