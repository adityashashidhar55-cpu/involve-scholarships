Cameron Applied Science Scholarship

Established by Hugh Cameron, B.Sc. 1973, and Heather Hume, B.A. 1972, M.D. 1978. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence to a student in third or fourth year of any program in Smith Engineering who has applied his or her engineering knowledge and/or techniques in an innovative manner related to non-traditional engineering fields. Nominations may be made by faculty members or students should apply by letter, with attached resume, to Smith Engineering by October 1.
