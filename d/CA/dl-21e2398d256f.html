Robert B. Peterson Admission Scholarship

Established in October 2003 by colleagues and friends of Robert B. Peterson (BSc 1959, MSc 1961), former Chair, CEO, and President of Imperial Oil and his extraordinary leadership during the Campaign for Queen's (1996-2003). Last revised in April 2025 and awarded on the basis of academic excellence to students entering first year of any first-entry undergraduate degree program. Preference will be given to students from the provinces of Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta or British Columbia. The award is renewable for three subsequent years as per the renewable awards policy.
