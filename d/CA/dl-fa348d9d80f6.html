Accenture Scholarship in the Smith School of Business

Established in May 2002 and last revised in March 2018 by Accenture Inc. and awarded on the basis of academic excellence, proven leadership skills, and involvement in school or community activities to students in their second year in the Bachelor of Commerce program in the Smith School of Business. Preference will be given to students who have had exposure to a consulting-type business or initiative, or who demonstrate a strong interest in consulting.
