Chloe Patricia Jean Bushie Memorial Scholarship

One scholarship is available for a full-time student in any year of study in any faculty, college or school at the University of Manitoba who has First Nation Treaty Status, has achieved a minimum degree point average or 2.5 and is involved in, or connected to, the indigenous culture in their community. Application Deadline: November 1st
