CHFT (Co-operative Housing Federation of Toronto) Scholarship Program

To be awarded to students selected by CHFT who have satisfactorily completed first year at the University of Toronto and who are registered as full time students in the subsequent academic year. The award will be provided for the second, third and fourth year of full time study.
