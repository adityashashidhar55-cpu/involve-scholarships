Commitment Scholar’s Award

The Commitment Scholars Award celebrates and recognizes demonstrated leadership in, and commitment to, racial justice, social justice, or diversity initiatives by a student in their high school or in their community. To be eligible students must identify as a member of an underserved or underrepresented group, including Black, Indigenous, and other racialized students, Persons with Disabilities, and 2SLGBTQ+ individuals.
