Douglas C. Cryderman Award

Established in April 2024 by the Estate of Douglas C. Cryderman, B.Sc.(Chemical Engineering) 1952. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to full-time students entering first year of a Bachelor of Applied Science degree program in Smith Engineering, whose address on admission to Queen's University is from the Township of South Stormont.
