Donald R. Dorrance Entrance Award

Established in January 2001 by Robert Dorrance, B. Sc. 1974 and Gail Drummond in honour of Donald R., B. Sc. (Eng) 1946, father of Robert Dorrance and Thomas Dorrance, B. Com. 1973, and husband of Stella Dorrance. Awarded on the basis of financial need and academic excellence to a student entering the first year program in Smith Engineering. The awards are open to Canadian citizens or permanent resident from Ontario or Quebec. Application should be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid and Awards by February 15.
