Commerce Society Conference Bursary

Established in January 2021 by the Commerce Society. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students enrolled in the Bachelor of Commerce degree program in the Smith School of Business. Funds are to be used to cover conference or competition fees. Application forms, including submission deadlines, are available through the Commerce Portal. Selection will be made by the Commerce Awards Committee in consultation with Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid and Awards.
