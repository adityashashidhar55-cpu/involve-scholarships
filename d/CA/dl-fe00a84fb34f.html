Ontario-Ukraine Solidarity Scholarship

The Ontario-Ukraine Solidarity Scholarship is available to all students (domestic and international, full-time and part-time) who are attending a publicly-assisted college or university in Ontario in the 2024-25, 2025-26, and/or 2026-27 academic years. Residen
