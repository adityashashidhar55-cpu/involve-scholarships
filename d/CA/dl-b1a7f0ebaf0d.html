Pat Douglas-Murray Memorial Scholarship

Established in November 1986 by friends and colleagues in memory of Pat (Osborough) Douglas-Murray (Arts 1955), who tutored students in FILM 110. Last revised in November 2025. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence to a student entering second year in a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) degree program with an academic plan offered by the Department of Film and Media.
