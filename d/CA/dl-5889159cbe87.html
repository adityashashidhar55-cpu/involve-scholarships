Justin Basinger Memorial Award

The Justin Basinger Memorial Award will be awarded to a student in the Faculty of Law in recognition of the best essay concerning Indigenous legal issues. The intent of the award is to encourage excellence in legal scholarship as it relates to Indigenous topic
