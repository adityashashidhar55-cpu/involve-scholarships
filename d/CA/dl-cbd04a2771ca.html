Louis Riel Bursaries at the University of Manitoba

Louis Riel Bursaries at the University of Manitoba Available to qualifying Red River Métis students with demonstrated financial need studying at the University of Manitoba. To be considered for the Louis Riel Bursary in the fall/winter academic session, eligible Red River Métis students MUST complete both the Louis Riel Bursary Application (available through the Riel Institute) and the University of Manitoba general bursary application.
