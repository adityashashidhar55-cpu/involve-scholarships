CIBC Future Heroes Bursary Program

Over the next two years, CIBC has committed $500,000 towards the CIBC Future Heroes Fund, a bursary program created as a tribute to the people who are bravely fighting COVID-19 and to support the next generation in their ambition of having a career in health care. For the 2021-22 academic year, 125 entrance bursaries will be available to students entering their first year of a first degree, be it a bachelor or diploma in an eligible health care program, at 50 select Canadian registered colleges or universities. Each bursary is valued at $2,500 CAD for a one-year award. Learn more and apply.
