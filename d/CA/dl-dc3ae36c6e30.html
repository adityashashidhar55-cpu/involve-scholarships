The Wilkin Family Award

Awarded to an undergraduate student with financial need from the indicated program, level and faculty following the order of priority: 1. A Commerce Entrance Award for a student entering Commerce from Brantford or Brant County (the “Area); if none, then 2. A Commerce Award for a student from the Area entering 2nd year; if none, then 3. A Commerce Award for a student from the Area entering 3rd year; if none, then 4. A Commerce Award for a student from the Area entering their final year of study; if none, then 5. A Masters Award for a student from the Area entering their 1st year of fulltime stu
