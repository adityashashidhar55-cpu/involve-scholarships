Doris Thompson Award

The friends of Doris Thompson established this award to be given to the student in the Fourth year of the Doctor of Pharmacy program who achieves the highest grade in Institutional Pharmacy Practice Management (PHM371H1), and who has been accepted into a hospital pharmacy residency program.
