University of Manitoba Entrance Bursary Application

This entrance bursary application will only be used to select recipients for the following bursaries (Asper Foundation and Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd.) for high school students entering their first year of study in University 1 or any direct entry faculty, college or school at the University of Manitoba. Please note that returning university students are not eligible to apply for these entrance bursaries.
