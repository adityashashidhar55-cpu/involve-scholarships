Kehoe Social Impact Award

Established in March 2022 by the Centre for Social Impact in the Smith School of Business with funds originally provided by Michael Kehoe, BCom'78, and Lynne Kehoe. Awarded on the basis of academic achievement in the required course(s) for the Certificate in Social Impact, engagement in and contributions to the Centre for Social Impact to students entering third or fourth year of a Bachelor of Commerce degree program who are enrolled in the Certificate in Social Impact. The Centre for Social Impact will make an application form available each spring for nominations, including self-nomination.
