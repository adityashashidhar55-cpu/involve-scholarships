Charles C. J. Lorimer Memorial Award

Established in memory of Dr. Charles C.J. Lorimer, B.Sc. '79, M.D.'83. Awarded to a student in an upper year of science, medicine or other Faculty who, while maintaining good academic standing, has shown an understanding of the partnership of people with the natural world in the preservation and enrichment of the vitality and beauty of the earth. The recipient will have demonstrated this participation in wilderness living, travel, hiking, canoeing, by involvement in conservation or by similar activities.
