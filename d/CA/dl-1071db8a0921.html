Lena ADAM Memorial Bursary for Students with Disabilities

Bursaries totalling $6,050 have been made available through an endowment established with a bequest from Lena Adam for students who have a physical disability and who demonstrate financial need. The award is adjudicated by the Committee on Awards for Students with Disabilities.
