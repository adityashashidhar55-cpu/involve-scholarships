Johnathan Andrew Marc Brown Award in Physics

Established in October 2011 by the Estates of John Brown, B.A. 1965, M.Ed. 1975, and Judith Brown, B.A. 1969. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need and academic achievement to students in any year of a B.Sc.(Honours) program in the Faculty of Arts and Science with preference given to students in a degree Plan in Physics.
