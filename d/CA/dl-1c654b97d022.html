E. A. ALM Bursary

Campus: Vancouver Required documentation: Submit documentation of Swedish descent (i.e. parents’ or grandparents’ birth certificate). A bursary of $500, gift of the Swedish Canadian Club, is awarded to a student beginning or continuing studies at the University of British Columbia. To be eligible for the award, applicants must: (a) be of Swedish descent, either through the father or the mother; and (b) have been resident in British Columbia for at least twelve months prior to the commencement of the academic term.
