Michele Mainland Memorial Scholarship in Chemical Engineering

Established in memory of Michele Mainland, B.Sc. 1997, by family, friends, and fellow students to honour Michele's love of learning and education. Two awards are given annually to Chemical Engineering students completing second and third year with the highest overall standing on their year's work.
