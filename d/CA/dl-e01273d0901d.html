Ted Reeve Memorial Award

Established by friends and associates of Ted Reeve, who was a football coach and a long-time friend of Queen's. Awarded to upper-year students to recognize outstanding academic achievement and demonstrated qualities of courage, team loyalty, and fair play on an intercollegiate team. Preference will be given to students who play on the Queen's Golden Gaels Football team. The maximum amount of each award will be according to OUA regulations and will adhere to all other OUA and U SPORT regulations for Athletics Awards.
