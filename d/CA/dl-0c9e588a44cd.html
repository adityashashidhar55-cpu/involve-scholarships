Ian T. CAMERON Memorial Bursary

Campus:Vancouver Required documentation: Submit documentation of participation in the Junior Forest Warden program, Boy Scouts or Girl Guides. A $1,250 bursary, in memory of Ian T. Cameron, is made available each year to a needy student in the Faculty of Forestry. Preference is given to students who have been active in the Junior Forest Warden program, Boy Scouts or Girl Guides.
