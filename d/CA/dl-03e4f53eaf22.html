George and Phyllis LONDON Bursary

Campus: Vancouver Required documentation: Submit documentation of pre-post-secondary residence (i.e. high school attendance). Bursaries totalling $1,850 have been endowed in honour of George and Phyllis London, lifetime residents and dairy farmers in the Delta, B.C. area. The award is offered to students in the Faculty of Land and Food Systems, pursuing a course leading to a career in the dairy industry. Preference is given to individuals ordinarily resident prior to post-secondary education, in the Delta or Fraser Valley area.
