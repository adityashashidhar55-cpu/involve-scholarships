Premier's Healthy Living Scholarship for Youth

The Premier’s Healthy Living Scholarship for Youth recognizes the achievements of young Manitobans who are positive role models for other children and youth in promoting healthy lifestyles. Up to 10 of the most outstanding applicants from across Manitoba will be selected to receive a $1,000 scholarship. Deadline date for applications is August 1. Download the application
