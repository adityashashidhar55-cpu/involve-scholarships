Fred Euringer Award in Drama

Established by colleagues and friends to honour the long and distinguished career of J.A. (Fred) Euringer, actor, director, playwright and teacher, and for many years the Head of the Department of Drama. Awarded annually to a student enrolled in a Queen's University Drama program and is intended to facilitate the recipient's ability to create theatre within the Kingston community. Applications will available from the DAN School of Drama and Music and must be submitted by March 31. A covering letter outlining the proposed activity for which the award is to be used as well as a curriculum vitae
