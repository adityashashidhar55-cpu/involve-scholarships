George H. Newsome Award in Mining Engineering

Established in December 2001 in memory of George H. Newsome (B.Sc. 1939) by his wife, Mrs. Janet Newsome, and awarded on the basis of financial need and academic merit to a full-time student in the second, third, or fourth year of the Mining Engineering program in Smith Engineering. Application should be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by October 31. Value: variable.
