Aileen M. Truesdell Memorial Award

Established by Nancy Fowler, B.A. 1998 and Robert Robson in memory of Aileen M. Truesdell, singer, dancer and pianist, whose musical talents and performances influenced family, friends and the community. Awarded on the basis of financial need and achievement to a full-time, upper year student in a Bachelor of Music program in the DAN School of Drama and Music, who has demonstrated commitment to music outside of the university music program through extra-curricular activities or community involvement.
