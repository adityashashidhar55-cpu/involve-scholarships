Robert & Jane (Halliley) Clapp Admission Bursary

Established in March 2018 by Robert Clapp, B.Sc. 1964 and Jane (Halliley) Clapp, B.A. 1964. Awarded on the basis of demonstrated financial need to students entering the first year of any first-entry undergraduate degree program in the Faculty of Arts and Science or Smith Engineering. The award is renewable for three subsequent years provided a satisfactory academic average is maintained each year and financial need remains evident. Applications are to be made to the Office of the University Registrar, Financial Aid, and Awards by February 15. Value: $2,500 x 4 years.
