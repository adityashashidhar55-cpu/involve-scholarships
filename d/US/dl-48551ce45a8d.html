CIAC Tradition of Excellence Scholarship

The CIAC's Tradition of Excellence scholarships provide tuition assistance to undergraduate students at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln Durham School of Architectural Engineering and Construction (DSAEC) pursuing careers in the construction industry.
