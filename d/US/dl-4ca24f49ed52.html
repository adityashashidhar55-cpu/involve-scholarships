Against All Odds Scholarship 2026

Life is rarely fair, and some people can be faced with more challenges than others. Adjusting to one’s circumstances and finding a way to overcome these challenges can be difficult, but not impossible. Adams & Bischoff would like to extend a $1,000 scholarship to a student with the best 750-word essay discussing obstacles that they have had to overcome in their life, and how they persevered despite those challenges.
