Michael Moody Fitness Scholarship

The Michael Moody Fitness Scholarship offers $1,500 to a high school senior, undergraduate, or graduate student who has demonstrated outstanding achievement, participation, leadership in school activities and work experience, and interest in pursuing a career in the health and fitness-related fields. The student may apply and use the money for education-related expenses, including tuition, fees, books, supplies, room and board. Michael Moody Fitness sponsors the scholarship. The 2025 program will accept scholarship applications from August 1st, 2025 until 1,000 are submitted or August 1st, 2026, whichever comes first. Your application \*must\* be one of the first 1,000 submitted by the deadline.
