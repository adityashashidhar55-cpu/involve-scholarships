Samuel S. Wilks Memorial Award

To honor a distinguished individual who has made statistical contributions to the advancement of scientific or technical knowledge, ingenious application of existing knowledge, or successful activity in the fostering of cooperative scientific efforts that have been directly involved in matters of national defense or public interest. It recognizes outstanding contributions to statistics that carry on in the spirit of his work.
