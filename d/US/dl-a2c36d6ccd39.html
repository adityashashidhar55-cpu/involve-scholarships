Jerry Dominguez Scholarship Fund

The Jerry Dominguez Scholarship fund was created to invest in our future business leaders and entrepreneurs. This scholarship is for graduating high school seniors pursuing a business career at a 4-year college/university or accredited vocational school. Each year, Business Consumer Alliance (BCA) will award five recipients a scholarship with an award amount of $10,000 each. Must be a high school senior residing in the following southern California counties: Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, and San Bernardino counties.
