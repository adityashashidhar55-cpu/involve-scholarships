Dare to Dream Scholarship

The Dare to Dream Scholarship by The Law Office of Lloyd E. Bennett, Esq. P.C. is offering a $500 scholarship to a student who shares their unique journey towards pursuing American citizenship. This scholarship celebrates the aspirations of individuals pursuing the American Dream. Applicants are invited to reflect on their family background, personal goals, and how citizenship can help bring those dreams to life.
