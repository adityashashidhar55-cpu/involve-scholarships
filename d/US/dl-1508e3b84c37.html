Amy G Injury Firm Access to Education Scholarship

The attorneys of the Amy G Injury Firm believe in the notion that everyone is entitled to an equal opportunity. The Amy G Injury Firm Access to Education Scholarship asks students to offer innovative and thought-provoking ideas for expanding higher education access and strong articulation as to why they believe that objective is so critically important to the future of our nation and the world.
