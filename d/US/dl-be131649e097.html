Northwest Pipe Company Trade School Scholarship Program

Northwest Pipe Company is offering up to six $2,500 scholarships to eligible students residing within 50 miles of its facilities in California, Missouri, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Washington, or West Virginia. Applicants must be high school seniors or graduates who plan to enroll in full-time study at an accredited vocational-technical school in their state for the upcoming academic year. To qualify, students must be pursuing certification as a welder, electrician, industrial mechanic, or mobile mechanic.   Awards are not renewable; however, students may reapply to the program each year they meet eligibility  requirements.
