Legends Global Scholarship Program

The Legends Global Scholarship Program reflects our commitment to fostering equity by expanding access to higher education and meaningful career exposure in the communities we serve. By helping to remove financial barriers and creating opportunities for engagement, we aim to support students in reaching their full potential and contributing positively to their communities. This year, through the Legends Global Acts Foundation, we are awarding $3,000 scholarships to up to 40 students residing in cities where we operate around the world.
