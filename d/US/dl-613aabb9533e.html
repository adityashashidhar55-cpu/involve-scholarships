Animal Rescue Scholarship

Aulsbrook Car & Truck Wreck Lawyers is proud to announce the \*Animal Rescue Scholarship\*, a \*$2,500\* award created to recognize students who are passionate about animal welfare and have dedicated their time to supporting rescue animals or animal welfare organizations. Attorney Matthew Aulsbrook is an active dog foster who believes every rescue animal deserves a safe and loving home. Inspired by the compassion shown by volunteers, advocates, and animal rescue organizations across the country, the firm created this scholarship to support students who share that commitment while pursuing their educational goals.
