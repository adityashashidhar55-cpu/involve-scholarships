Engineering and Construction Contracting (ECC) Association Scholarship

Students seeking a degree or certification in Engineering, Construction Management, or an eligible skilled trade or craft can earn scholarships ranging from $2,000 to $5,000 from the Engineering and Construction Contracting (ECC) Association Scholarship Program.  The ECC Association is committed to awarding up to $60,000 in total scholarships, with the number and value of individual awards determined by the number of qualified applicants and the strength of their applications.    Up to thirty (30) scholarships may be granted, including:  Up to ten (10) awards for students pursuing a degree in Engineering  Up to ten (10) awards for students pursuing a degree in Construction Management  Up to ten (10) awards for students pursuing a certification in a skilled trade or craft (see application site for a full listing of eligible fields)
