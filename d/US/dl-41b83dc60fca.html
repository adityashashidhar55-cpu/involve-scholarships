Boyk Law Veteran Scholarship

The scholarship was created as part of Boyk Law’s continued commitment to the servicemembers of the United States Armed Forces and their continuing education. The 2026 Boyk Law Veteran Scholarship will award $2,000 to a student currently attending an accredited college or university who has either served in the Armed Forces or is the child of a US Veteran.
