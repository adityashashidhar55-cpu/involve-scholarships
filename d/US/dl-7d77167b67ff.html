Fred S. Findling Memorial Immigrant Scholarship

The Fred S. Findling Memorial Immigrant Scholarship was established by The Probate Pro in honor of founder Darren Findling's father—a Holocaust survivor who immigrated to the U.S.—to support undergraduate immigrant students pursuing their own American Dream. Rooted in a deep respect for every individual's unique story and potential, this scholarship encourages hardworking students from immigrant backgrounds to apply.
