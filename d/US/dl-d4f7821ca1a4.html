Rebuilding the Road Ahead: How an Accident Inspired My New Direction Scholarship

You are eligible to apply for the Rebuilding the Road Ahead scholarship if you are an undergraduate or graduate student who meets these criteria: \* Admitted to or enrolled in an accredited university, college, or graduate school in the U.S. \* High school seniors can apply as well, if they can provide proof that they were accepted to an accredited college/university in the U.S. \* 3.0 GPA (must include unofficial transcript) \* Be a U.S. citizen or otherwise authorized to work or attend school in the U.S. \* Create a video on the proposed topic and submit the link through the form \* Apply using the form on this page \* Agree to the Terms and Conditions \* Were involved in a car accident Students from all areas of study are eligible to apply, provided they meet the scholarship eligibility requirements outlined above. High school seniors must make sure to provide proof that they were accepted to an accredited university or college (such as a letter of acceptance). All interested students who meet the eligibility criteria must complete the application form on this page and include a link to their scholarship video.
