Replace Industrial Scholarship

Replace Industrial is awarding a $500 scholarship to an undergraduate or trade school student who submits a 750-1,000 word essay answering the given year's essay prompt. The prompt changes each year, and each topic relates to small business, USA manufacturing, and local businesses. The winner of the scholarship will have the $500 award sent directly to the financial aid office of the winner's institution. Applicants must be over the age of 18. Only one entry per applicant per contest. Replace Industrial reserves the right to use the content of all applications for marketing purposes. Submit your application today to earn a scholarship and show us how much small business means to you! The 2025 Prompt: Why are you passionate about small, locally owned business? Do you have any experience on how you or your family have been positively impacted by a small, local business?
