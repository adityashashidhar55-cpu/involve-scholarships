Injury Advocacy Scholarship

At Schlack & Bassmaji, we understand how a serious injury can uproot your life. The medical bills, lost wages, and recovery time can all take a huge toll on yourself and those that you love. It’s important to be as best prepared as you can for these unexpected situations, but that isn’t the case most of the time. With injuries prevalent everywhere–on the street, in the workplace, even at home–we want to hear your experiences. It’s essential to raise awareness of the prevalence of injuries caused by someone else’s negligence.  This is why we are offering the 2026 Injury Advocacy Scholarship, aimed to bring attention to injuries that happened due to the fault of another. Submit your application and share your story before the September 1, 2026 deadline to be considered for this opportunity.
