Pi Delta Phi French Scholarship

Undergraduate: To allow undergraduate regular members of an active Pi Delta Phi chapter to continue their studies of the French language and the literatures and cultures of the French-speaking world. Graduate: To allow a full-time graduate student who is a member of an active Pi Delta Phi chapter and who is pursuing graduate work in French or select related fields to study or to conduct research abroad in a French-speaking country.
