Society of Yeager Scholars for Marshal University

The Yeager Scholarship Program at Marshall University offers a comprehensive full-ride scholarship for academically exceptional students, providing full tuition, room and board, study opportunities at Oxford, a personal computer, and a living stipend. The scholarship is open to all first-time, full-time freshmen in all majors. Yeager Scholars are immersed in a challenging curriculum, leadership development, and community engagement, maintaining a 3.5 GPA and contributing to campus life. Students are recommended to have a 30 ACT, 1360 SAT and a 3.5 high school gpa.
