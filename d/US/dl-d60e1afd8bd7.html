Corvias Foundation Military Spouse Scholarship

Military spouses pursuing higher education, professional licensure, certifications, and career advancement opportunities are eligible to apply. Funds may be used for tuition, course or lab fees, program or other costs of attendance, program fees or exam costs/fees, professional expenses such as licensure/certification (or renewal), CEU's, professional examinations and other related expenses such as fingerprinting and background check fees, or clinical supervision of mental health license hours.
