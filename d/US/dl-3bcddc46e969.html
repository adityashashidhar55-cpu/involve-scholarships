Albers-Alexander LGBTQ Law Scholarship

The Albers-Alexander LGBTQ Law Scholarship Fund was created to reduce barriers to legal education and expand opportunities for LGBTQ law students who demonstrate leadership, resilience, and financial need. By supporting students who are often underrepresented in higher educationincluding LGBTQ students of color, transgender students, immigrants, first-generation college students, and those from low-income or geographically isolated backgroundsthe scholarship aims to cultivate the next generation of legal advocates advancing equity and justice for the LGBTQ community.
