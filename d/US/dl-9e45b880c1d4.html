Dixie Belle Paint Co. Scholarship

How has creativity helped you in problem solving? (500 words or less). To enter, beginning October 31, 2023, submit a complete scholarship Application online at the Sponsors Website (https://dixiebellepaint.com/creativity-le ads-to-success-scholarship/) no later than June 15, 2024 at 11:59 pm Pacific Time. Limit one (1) Application per person. Application must include name and contact information, background details, academic details, and a short original, previously unpublished written response to the following topic, How has creativity helped you in problem solving?
