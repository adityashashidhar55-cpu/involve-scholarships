Purple Tassel College Scholarship

Corvias Foundation, a nonprofit supporting the educational goals of military-connected families, is offering up to $20,000 in awards to high school seniors from active-duty military families. The announcement kicks off the Foundations year-long 20th anniversary celebration, commemorating two decades of educational support and opportunities. Since its founding in 2006, Corvias Foundation has awarded more than $20 million in scholarships to military families and children of Corvias team members.
