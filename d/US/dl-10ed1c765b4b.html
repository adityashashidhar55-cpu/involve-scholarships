Colgate-Palmolive Scholars Program

The Colgate-Palmolive Scholars Program aims to provide financial support and mentorship to students who are pursuing careers in the growing healthcare industry. The program is intended to help support the development of a talent pool for the healthcare industry, while providing for a clear pathway for individuals who want to thrive in the industry.
