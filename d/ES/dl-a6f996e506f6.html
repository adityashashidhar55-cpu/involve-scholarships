IESE and ONCE Foundation Scholarships

IESE and the ONCE Foundation grant unique annual scholarships of up to 65% of tuition fees to people with disabilities who wish to study the Executive MBA. The scholarships are offered to people with a legally recognized disability of at least 33%, who must present a copy of their disability certificate and fill out a scholarship application form in order to be evaluated.
