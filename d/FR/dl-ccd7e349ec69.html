"Horizon" Scholarship (Excellence scholarship at Master 2 level from the University of La Réunion for students from the Indian Ocean area)

The University of La Réunion (UR) is offering 9 excellence scholarships for students with a M1 or M2 degree from universities in the Indian Ocean who wish to follow one of the 25 Master 2 courses open to the programme and, if possible, pursue a doctoral thesis.
