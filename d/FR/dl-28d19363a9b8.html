Internship grant for international master students in Geosciences, Climate, Environment, Planets

The Graduate School “Geosciences, Climate, Environment, Planets” of Paris-Saclay University provides financial support to international Master students willing to conduct a research internship within one of its laboratory (i.e., GEOPS, LSCE, LATMOS, CEARC, ECOSYS, DORA, DPHY).
