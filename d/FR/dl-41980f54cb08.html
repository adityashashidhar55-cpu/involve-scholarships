EDHEC Academic Excellence Scholarship

The École des Hautes Études Commerciales (EDHEC) Academic Excellence Scholarship offers high-potential candidates up to 50% reduction on their tuition fees. The scholarship supports high-profile candidates based on the excellence of their academic profile, impressive experience and extracurricular activities.
