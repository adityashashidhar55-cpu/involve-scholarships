Master 2 Scholarship of the Grenoble Alliance for Integrated Structural & Cell Biology

The Grenoble Alliance for Integrated Structural & Cell Biology (GRAL) funds Master 2 research scholarships. Laureates will attend the 2nd year of Master of Biology, Plant Biology, or Health Engineering at Université Grenoble Alpes, and do a research internship in one of the GRAL labs in Grenoble.
