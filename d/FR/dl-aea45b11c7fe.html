DFF-International Postdoctoral Travel Grant - Independent Research Fund Denmark

The DFF-International Postdoctoral Travel Grant offers up to 1,500,000 DKK for international research stays. Applicants must have obtained a PhD from a Danish institution or be about to defend their thesis within 12 months after the application deadline.
