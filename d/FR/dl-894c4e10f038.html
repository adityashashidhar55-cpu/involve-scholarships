Graduate School Masters Scholarships - Grenoble Alpes University

The structuring and transdisciplinary program of the Graduate School finances Master's scholarships for students coming to study in one of the Master's programs belonging to a thematic program of the University Grenoble Alpes. The scholarships are intended for students who do not hold a French baccalaureate.
